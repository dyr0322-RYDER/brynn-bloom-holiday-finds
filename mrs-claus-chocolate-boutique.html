CHRISTMAS / TEACHER GIFTS / EASY DIY

Mrs. Claus's Chocolate Boutique

Turn a simple six-egg carton into an adorable little Christmas chocolate boutique! Six individually wrapped chocolates, vintage Christmas paper and a gingham bow make a thoughtful, lightweight teacher gift.

MAKE IT YOURSELF

Six easy steps

  1. Choose a new six-egg carton. Use a brand-new, unused carton with a secure lid, never one previously used for eggs.
  2. Dress up the lid. Cut vintage red-and-cream Christmas scrapbook paper to fit the top, leaving the hinge and closure free. Attach with double-sided tape or craft glue.
  3. Add the boutique sign. Make a cream scalloped label that reads “Mrs. Claus's Chocolate Boutique.” Add a little holly or a vintage-style Santa illustration.
  4. Arrange six chocolates. Place factory-wrapped chocolates in appropriately sized mini paper candy cups, one per compartment. Make sure the carton closes without crushing them.
  5. Wrap with gingham ribbon. Tie a removable bow around the closed carton so the recipient can easily open it.
  6. Finish with a tiny flourish. Attach faux greenery or a miniature bell to the ribbon, away from the edible contents.

Budget for seven gifts

Plan for seven new cartons, 42 wrapped chocolates, a roll of gingham ribbon, printed labels, candy cups and optional embellishments. A reasonable unverified planning target is $25–$40 total for seven, before tax and shipping. Check multipack quantities and carton dimensions before ordering.

Food safety: Use new cartons only. Keep chocolates in their original wrappers with ingredient and allergen information available. Avoid glitter or loose decorations inside the carton, and check school policies or allergy restrictions.

Make it your own

Try a vintage red-and-cream paper lid, a wide red gingham bow and a little greenery. A 2.5 × 1.5-inch label is a good starting size, but measure your particular carton before printing.
