The Oversized Christmas Bow

Make one generous ribbon bow that refreshes the decorations you already have.

Original illustrated design concept; not a photograph of a completed project. The final appearance depends on the materials and technique you choose.

The creative shortcut: Start with what you own, change one finish or focal point, and repeat a coordinated detail. No expensive premade decoration required.
THE MAKEOVER

How to create the look

01

Choose a generous ribbon

Pick wired ribbon that holds its shape. Measure the length against your intended wreath, door or stair rail before cutting.

02

Make matching loops

Fold the ribbon into alternating loops, pinching the center firmly as you work.

03

Secure and shape

Wrap floral wire tightly around the center, add tails and fluff each loop until the bow feels balanced.

04

Move it around the house

Attach to an existing wreath, garland or gift basket. Use appropriate fasteners so the bow can be reused next season.
