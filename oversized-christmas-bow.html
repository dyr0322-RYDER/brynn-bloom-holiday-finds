SEASONAL MAGIC / THE EASY DIY EDIT

One Oversized Christmas Bow That Refreshes Everything

A generous wired-ribbon bow can transform a wreath, garland or gift without replacing the decoration.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Choose your ribbon. Use wide wired ribbon that holds its shape; measure against the decoration.
  2. Form the loops. Fold alternating ribbon loops and pinch the center.
  3. Secure the middle. Wrap floral wire firmly around the center, then cut two generous tails.
  4. Fluff and reuse. Shape each loop and attach with suitable fasteners to a wreath, garland or gift.

Photograph is styling inspiration, not an exact depiction of the finished DIY. The final look depends on your materials. Keep décor away from flames and heat; use flameless candles where applicable.
