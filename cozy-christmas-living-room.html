The Cozy Christmas Living Room

Small changes that create a festive feel without new furniture.

Inspiration photo: Valeria Boltneva / Pexels. Not a photograph of a product being sold.

Before buying: Compare the listing’s dimensions, materials, quantity, weather suitability and delivery terms. A styled photograph may feature more than one package of greenery, ribbon or ornaments.
STEP 01

Choose a single focal point

Select a mantel, side table or bookshelf to carry most of the seasonal styling. One styled area has more impact than scattered decorations on every surface.

STEP 02

Swap covers, not inserts

If you already have pillow inserts, check the measurements and buy only seasonal covers. Two coordinated covers often make a stronger statement than a pile of unrelated pillows.

STEP 03

Layer texture

A neutral throw with a textured knit works across several holiday color schemes. Try what you already own before purchasing an extra blanket.

STEP 04

Borrow color from the tree

Repeat a little evergreen or burgundy in nearby ribbons, cushions or candleholders. Matching the undertone matters more than buying everything from one collection.

STEP 05

Use safe light

Battery-operated candles work well where open flames could be a problem. Check battery type, timer function and dimensions before buying.
