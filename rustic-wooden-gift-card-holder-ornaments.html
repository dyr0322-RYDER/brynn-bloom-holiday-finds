I Turned Scrap Fabric and Wooden Ornaments into Beautiful Christmas Gift Card Holders!

Make a gift card feel handmade and personal with a rustic wood ornament and a little scrap fabric. A sturdy plaid or cream pocket holds the card while jute, evergreen and berries turn the holder into a keepsake for the tree.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the gift card holder ornaments

  1. Start with a wide ornament. Choose a flat, unfinished round wood ornament about 4½–5 inches across. A standard gift card is approximately 3⅜ × 2⅛ inches, so a tiny ornament will not work. If the blank has no hanging hole, drill a small hole near the top with suitable tools and eye protection.
  2. Cut the fabric pocket. Use a scrap of sturdy buffalo plaid or cream cotton canvas. For a simple sewn pocket, cut a rectangle approximately 4¼ × 3¼ inches; fold the upper long edge under ¼ inch twice and stitch to finish the opening. Fold the remaining three edges under about ¼ inch and press. Test the card against the ornament before attaching.
  3. Attach the pocket. Center the pocket across the lower portion of the ornament with its top edge open. Stitch it to a separate fabric backing if desired, or use fabric-and-wood-compatible glue along only the left, right and bottom folded edges. Keep adhesive away from the opening. Let it cure fully according to its label, then slide a gift card in to confirm the pocket holds it securely without bending.
  4. Add rustic details. Tie a small burlap or plaid bow above the pocket. Tuck in a short evergreen sprig and a few red berries. Add a mini wooden snowflake or star if you like, leaving enough room to insert and remove the card.
  5. Make the hanger. Thread natural jute twine through the top hole and tie a secure loop. If desired, lightly sand rough wood edges first; keep the wood natural or apply a thin, fully dried coat of craft paint.
  6. Fill and gift. Insert a gift card into the pocket with its top edge visible. Hang the ornament on a Christmas tree, attach it to a wrapped present or use it as a thoughtful teacher or coworker gift. After the card is removed, the ornament can be reused every Christmas.

Make a batch without spending much

Budget tip: Cut several pockets from one fat quarter or leftover sewing scraps. Buy wood blanks in a pack if making teacher, coworker or stocking-stuffer gifts. Reuse tiny greenery clippings and ribbon offcuts from other projects.

Fit check: Gift cards vary slightly. Test with the actual card before gluing or sewing a whole batch. The card should slide in easily, stay upright and remain removable.

Safety: Sand splinters, let glue cure fully, and keep small embellishments away from young children and pets. These are decorative gift holders, not toys.
