DIY Vintage Christmas Ornaments with Burgundy Velvet Bows

Make a coordinated set of antique-gold-look ornaments from lightweight baubles, layered metallic paint and little burgundy velvet bows. Display them on your tree or box a few as a handmade gift.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in five steps

  1. Choose and prep. Use lightweight shatterproof ornaments in smooth or pre-textured styles. Remove dust, check that the caps are secure, and follow the paint maker’s surface-preparation directions. For the embossed look in the image, choose ornaments with raised designs or add lightweight decorative appliqués that are compatible with the ornament surface.
  2. Build the antique-gold finish. Dab compatible gold metallic craft paint onto the ornament with a foam applicator, working in thin layers. Let each coat dry as directed. A pre-gold ornament may need only a few accents.
  3. Add vintage texture. Lightly stipple a little bronze or dark antique-finish paint in irregular patches, especially around raised details, then gently add gold highlights. Test on a spare ornament first. Do not cover the ornament hanger or cap opening with paint.
  4. Make the velvet bow. Tie a small bow from narrow burgundy velvet ribbon, leaving short tails. Secure it near the cap with a little suitable craft glue or a thin tie, taking care not to loosen the hanger.
  5. Hang or gift. Add a matching velvet hanging loop or use the ornament’s original hanger. Let glue and paint cure fully before displaying. Arrange several together or package them in a protective gift box.

Use paint and adhesives labeled for your ornament material, follow ventilation and drying instructions, and use low-temperature glue only where suitable. The ornate raised designs in the concept image require pre-textured ornaments or separate embellishments; a sponge-painted smooth ball will have a distressed finish but not embossed patterns.
