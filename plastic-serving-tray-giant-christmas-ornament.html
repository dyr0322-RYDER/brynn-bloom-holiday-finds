Plastic Serving Tray → Giant Christmas Ornament

Turn an inexpensive round plastic serving tray into oversized vintage-style Christmas decor with paint, a simple ornament cap, velvet ribbon, greenery, berries and bells.

THE TRANSFORMATION

How to make it

  1. Choose a round plastic serving tray. A lightweight tray roughly 15–18 inches creates a large statement piece.
  2. Prep and paint. Clean and dry it, then use paint suitable for plastic. Add a second coat and lightly distress the edges if you want a vintage finish.
  3. Create the ornament cap. Use a small lightweight craft box or dense foam block. Paint it antique gold and attach a jute hanging loop before securing it to the top edge of the tray.
  4. Add the design. Use a stencil, paint or permanent adhesive vinyl to add a Merry Christmas design or snowflakes. Let finishes cure fully.
  5. Add greenery. Arrange faux cedar, berries and small pinecones around the ornament cap.
  6. Add the bow. Attach a generous wired velvet bow over the greenery.
  7. Finish with bells. Add lightweight jingle bells or other small embellishments.
  8. Hang securely. Display on a door, wall or mantel using hardware appropriate for the finished weight and surface.

Important: Keep the cap and embellishments lightweight. Test the hanging loop and all adhesive connections before hanging. For outdoor use, choose weather-appropriate paint, adhesive, ribbon and greenery and use a protected location.

Why this works

The round tray already provides the oversized ornament body. Adding a lightweight cap at the top changes the silhouette immediately, while paint and layered Christmas embellishments hide the humble starting point.
