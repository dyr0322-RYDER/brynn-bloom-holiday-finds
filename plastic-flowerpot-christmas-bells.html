Plastic Flowerpots → Oversized Christmas Bells

Turn ordinary plastic flowerpots into dramatic antique-gold Christmas bells using paint, rope, wooden beads, greenery and velvet ribbon.

How to make them

  1. Choose three plastic flowerpots in different sizes. Center drainage holes make assembly easiest.
  2. Clean and paint with antique-gold or bronze paint suitable for plastic.
  3. Create the clapper. Thread jute rope through the drainage hole and attach a large wooden bead so it hangs below the rim.
  4. Create the hanger. Leave enough rope above the upside-down pot for a loop and knot securely.
  5. Add greenery around the top with faux cedar, berries and pinecones.
  6. Add a velvet bow over the greenery.
  7. Distress the finish if desired for an aged-metal look.
  8. Display together at staggered heights.

Important: These are decorative bells. Test all knots and hanging points before displaying overhead. Use weather-appropriate materials for protected outdoor displays.

Why this works

The flowerpot itself becomes the bell body. The existing drainage hole provides the path for the rope and wooden-bead clapper, so no hidden structure is needed.
