I Turned a Plain Mason Jar into the Cutest Snowman Gift!

One clean jar, mini marshmallows and a few scraps of felt and plaid fabric become an adorable Christmas gift for teachers, neighbors and friends. The little top hat makes the reveal—without requiring a fancy container.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make your snowman gift in six steps

  1. Prepare the jar. Wash a food-safe mason jar and its lid, rinse well, and dry completely. A straight-sided jar is easiest to decorate. Check that the lid and seal work before adding anything.
  2. Fill it with snow. With clean, dry hands or a food-safe scoop, add fresh mini marshmallows. Leave enough room to close the lid without crushing the contents. Close the jar firmly. Keep all glue, paint and craft decorations outside the sealed jar.
  3. Give your snowman a face. Cut small circles from black adhesive vinyl or removable stickers for eyes and smile. Cut a tiny carrot-shaped nose from orange vinyl or cardstock and attach it to the outside glass. The dots are decorations—not candy mixed into the marshmallows.
  4. Wrap the scarf. Cut a narrow strip of red-and-black plaid fabric or ribbon, tie it around the lower middle of the jar and trim the ends. Attach three black buttons to the outside with craft adhesive if desired. Let adhesive cure before gifting.
  5. Make the top hat. Cut a black felt rectangle for the hat's side, a circle for the top and a larger circle for the brim. Roll the rectangle into a short cylinder and glue its seam, then attach the top and brim. Wrap the crown with a narrow plaid ribbon. Glue the finished hat to a separate paper circle or removable decorative band resting on the lid, not to the jar's sealing threads. Allow it to dry fully.
  6. Add the finishing touch. Tuck a small faux greenery sprig into the hat band, then tie on a paper tag reading “There's SNOW one like you!” Include a note identifying the marshmallows and their ingredients/allergens from the original packaging. Store according to the marshmallow package and gift while fresh.

Make it even easier

Fastest version: Skip the constructed felt hat and tie a black ribbon bow around the lid. Big after reveal: Build the felt top hat, add the plaid scarf and attach a tiny thank-you tag. Use a jar you already own if it is food-safe, clean and has an undamaged lid.

Food safety: Do not place felt, paint, buttons, glitter, adhesive or greenery inside the jar. The decorations are not edible. This is a gift container, not a toy; small detachable embellishments are a choking hazard. If recipients have dietary restrictions, use an appropriate packaged alternative to marshmallows.
