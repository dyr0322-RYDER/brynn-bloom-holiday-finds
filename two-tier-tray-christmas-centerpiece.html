I Turned a Simple Two-Tier Tray into a Beautiful Christmas Centerpiece!

That everyday rustic tray can become a warm holiday focal point using miniature decorations, a plaid-and-burlap bow, evergreen and a few tiny lights. Use a tray you already own and shop your Christmas bins before buying a thing.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Style your Christmas tiered tray

  1. Start with the right tray. The pictured base is a two-tier round rustic wood tray with a black metal center post and ring handle. Measure each tier and the height between them. If you own a similar tray, reuse it instead of buying another.
  2. Anchor the upper tier. Place a small black metal-and-glass lantern with an ivory battery LED candle toward the back left. Lean a small round cream “Merry Christmas” sign beside it, leaving the front visible.
  3. Create the lower village. Set a miniature white house on the lower tier, with a snowy green bottle-brush tree on one side and a small brown reindeer on the other. Add a second little LED votive if space permits.
  4. Fill with greenery. Tuck short evergreen picks around the bases of the ornaments on both levels. Add glossy deep-red berry picks and a few small snow-dusted pinecones. Keep the greenery below the edge of the sign and away from the lantern door.
  5. Make the statement bow. Layer loops of red-and-black buffalo plaid wired ribbon with natural burlap ribbon. Tie the bow just below the tray's ring handle with twine or floral wire. Add a small evergreen sprig and a few berries behind it.
  6. Give it a soft glow. Weave a short warm-white battery fairy-light strand between the ornaments and greenery. Keep the battery pack accessible and the wires clear of sharp edges or moving parts.
  7. Balance the finished look. Step back and check that the two tiers feel full without hiding the house, deer, lantern or sign. Display on a stable table or kitchen island and change out the small decorations for other seasons later.

Make the most of what you have

Budget tip: The tray is the biggest investment if you don't already own one. Try an existing tiered tray, small ornaments, ribbon offcuts and greenery from old garland. You don't need to buy a new pack of every accent to recreate the look.

Safety: Use battery-operated LED lights and candles only, never an open flame beside ribbon or greenery. Don't overload the upper tier or block the center handle. Keep button batteries and small embellishments away from children and pets.
