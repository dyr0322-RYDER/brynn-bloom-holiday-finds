I Turned a Thrifted Lantern into a Gorgeous Christmas Centerpiece!

A plain black lantern gets an entirely new look with two glowing flameless candles, warm fairy lights, a lush burgundy-and-burlap bow, and little evergreen details. Reuse a lantern you already own or find one secondhand for the biggest savings.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the lantern centerpiece

  1. Find the right lantern. Choose a tall, black, rectangular metal-and-glass lantern with a top ring handle, like the before image. Look at secondhand shops or use one you already have. Make sure its door opens and there is room for two LED candles of different heights.
  2. Clean and plan. Wipe the glass and frame. Test where the taller and shorter flameless candles will sit. Keep the door accessible so you can turn the lights on and replace batteries.
  3. Build the glowing base. Set the battery-operated ivory LED pillar candles inside. Tuck a short warm-white battery fairy-light strand around the base, keeping the battery pack reachable. Add a few small faux pine sprigs, berries and frosted pinecones without blocking the door.
  4. Layer the ribbon. Make generous loops with wide deep-burgundy velvet wired ribbon and natural burlap ribbon. Shape a full layered bow and tie its center with jute twine or floral wire.
  5. Dress the top. Secure the bow to the lantern's top near the ring handle. Tuck short evergreen branches, red berry picks and two or three mini frosted pinecones behind the bow, distributing the greenery across the top like the photo. Keep the lantern's door and ring usable.
  6. Add a personal tag. Tie on a small kraft tag with a favorite holiday phrase. The image suggests “It's the most wonderful time of the year,” but a handwritten family name or “Merry Christmas” works too. Place the lantern on a stable mantel, hearth or table away from heat.

Get the look without buying everything

Start with the lantern you have. The biggest expense is usually the base lantern. Reuse last year's faux greenery, ribbon scraps, pinecones and battery candles. One bundle of picks can decorate more than one project.

Important: Use battery-operated LED candles and lights only, never a real flame with ribbon, greenery or decorations. Keep small embellishments away from children and pets and avoid placing battery packs where they could overheat or get wet.
