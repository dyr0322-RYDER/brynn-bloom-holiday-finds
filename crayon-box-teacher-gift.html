Turn an Ordinary Crayon Box into a Beautiful Teacher Gift

The humble crayon box gets a festive makeover with a paper sleeve, a handwritten thank-you message and one generous bow. Keep the crayons inside, make the box reusable, and turn a small everyday supply into a thoughtful gift.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the gift in six easy steps

  1. Start with a box of crayons. Choose a standard cardboard crayon carton and keep the crayons in their original arrangement. Check the top flap: the gift will look like an open crayon holder rather than a closed retail package.
  2. Make the open-top holder. Carefully open the top flap and fold or trim it back. If the carton is too short or flimsy to match the concept, cut a strip of cardstock to wrap around its sides and back, leaving the top open. Secure it with double-sided tape. Make sure the crayons can still slide in and out.
  3. Add the plaid. Measure the box, cut black-and-white plaid scrapbook paper to fit its front and sides, and attach with double-sided tape or a glue stick. Keep the top opening clear. A strip of cardstock behind the paper helps the box hold its shape.
  4. Make the teacher message. Cut a small rectangle of black cardstock or chalkboard-look paper. Write “Teachers color the world a brighter place” with white and colorful paint pens, or print the phrase on paper and glue it to the front. Let ink dry before handling.
  5. Finish with ribbon. Wrap a little jute twine around the lower half. Tie a red ribbon bow at the top or attach it to the back so it does not cover the crayons. A tiny faux greenery sprig is optional; keep it securely attached.
  6. Add a thank-you tag. Write a personal note on a cardstock gift tag, or use an optional apple-shaped tag. Slide the crayons back in and check that the box stands upright. For a fuller gift, pair it with a handmade card from your child.

Make it your own

Fastest version: Keep the original carton intact, wrap it with plaid paper, add a black paper label and tie a ribbon around it. More dramatic after reveal: Reinforce the carton into an open-top holder so the colorful crayons peek above the label, as in the concept image.

The image headline says “$1 crayon box” as a creative example, not a verified current price. Buy crayons wherever they cost least; you do not need to buy the crayons through Amazon. Use age-appropriate crayons and avoid small detachable decorations if the gift may be handled by young children.
