I Turned a Simple Glass Hurricane Vase into a Stunning Christmas Centerpiece!

Give a plain clear vase a warm holiday glow with one flameless ivory candle, a little artificial snow, evergreen sprigs, red berries and a deep burgundy velvet bow. Reuse a vase you already own to make the transformation especially affordable.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make your glowing Christmas hurricane

  1. Choose the vase. Start with a clear, straight-sided glass hurricane vase with a gently flared rim, like the before image. Check that it is wide enough for the LED pillar and greenery, and wash and dry it.
  2. Build a snowy base. Add a shallow layer of white artificial craft snow to the bottom. Set a wide ivory battery-operated LED pillar candle in the center. Keep the candle’s switch and battery compartment accessible.
  3. Frame the candle. Tuck short faux pine sprigs around the candle’s base, then arrange small glossy red berry clusters and a few snow-dusted pinecones toward the front. Keep the candle visible.
  4. Add a warm sparkle. Weave a short warm-white battery fairy-light strand loosely through the greenery. Place its battery pack somewhere accessible without pinching the wire under the glass or concealing it in loose snow.
  5. Dress the rim. Layer a deep burgundy velvet bow with natural burlap ribbon. Add evergreen sprigs, red berries and one frosted pinecone at the top, securing the decoration around the outside of the rim with floral wire or twine. Do not glue to the glass if you want to reuse the vase.
  6. Finish the display. Set the hurricane on a natural wood slice or tray. Add a little greenery around its base and switch on the LEDs for a cozy holiday centerpiece. Keep the vase stable and the top decoration clear of the candle.

Keep the cost down

Shop your home first: use an existing hurricane vase, leftover faux garland, ribbon scraps, spare berries and a battery candle you already own. A wood slice is optional.

Safety: Use battery-operated LED lights and candles only—never an open flame in this decorated vase. Keep battery packs accessible and dry, avoid pinching wires, and keep small decorations and batteries away from children and pets.
