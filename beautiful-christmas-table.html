The Beautiful Christmas Table

An inviting table with room for dinner and conversation.

Inspiration photo: Elina Fairytale / Pexels. Not a photograph of a product being sold.

Before buying: Compare the listing’s dimensions, materials, quantity, weather suitability and delivery terms. A styled photograph may feature more than one package of greenery, ribbon or ornaments.
STEP 01

Begin with your everyday dishes

White, cream and clear glassware can anchor a Christmas table. Add only one seasonal color through napkins, ribbon or greenery.

STEP 02

Measure the table runner

Before buying, measure the table and decide whether you prefer an overhang. A runner that is too wide or too long may interfere with place settings.

STEP 03

Keep the centerpiece low

Guests should be able to see one another. Cluster a few pieces in the center and test the arrangement from a seated position.

STEP 04

Choose pieces with a second use

A plain tray, candleholders and washable runner can work through winter or for other occasions. That is more useful than a centerpiece labeled for one holiday only.

STEP 05

Test the complete layout

Set plates, glasses, serving dishes and seating before purchasing extra decorations. The most beautiful table still needs to function.
