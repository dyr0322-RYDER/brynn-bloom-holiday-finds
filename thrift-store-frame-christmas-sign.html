THRIFTED FIND / BIG CHRISTMAS AFTER

Turn a Thrift-Store Frame into a Glowing Christmas Sign

Give an inexpensive old frame a Christmas makeover with a free Brynn & Bloom printable, warm micro lights and a few holiday accents. Use what you already own, thrift the frame, and make the finished piece as simple or full as you like.

THE TRANSFORMATION

Make it in seven easy steps

  1. Find your frame. Look for an inexpensive thrift-store or secondhand frame with a shape you like. The concept image uses a distressed black frame, but wood, gold or white can work too.
  2. Clean and prep. Remove the backing and glass. Clean the frame well. Paint or distress it only if you want to change the finish.
  3. Print the artwork. Download the free Merry Christmas printable above. Print on white or warm-ivory cardstock and trim it to fit your frame opening.
  4. Add the glow. Arrange battery-operated warm-white micro fairy lights around the inside edge or behind the artwork. Keep the battery pack accessible on the back. Use lights intended for decorative indoor use and follow the manufacturer's instructions.
  5. Build the topper. Layer faux pine, a few berries and small pinecones at the top of the frame. Secure the pieces so they do not touch hot bulbs or block access to the battery pack.
  6. Add ribbon. Tie a red bow, buffalo-check bow, or layer the two. Attach it to the greenery with floral wire or craft adhesive.
  7. Finish and display. Replace the backing, hide the battery pack behind the frame, and set the sign on a mantel, console, shelf or entry table. Keep it indoors unless every material you use is specifically rated for outdoor use.

Make it for almost nothing

Use from home: an old frame, leftover ribbon, clipped faux greenery, pinecones and cardstock. The “$3 frame” shown in the concept is an example thrift-store find, not a guaranteed price.
