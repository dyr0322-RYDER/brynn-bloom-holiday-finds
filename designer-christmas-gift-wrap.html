Christmas Gift Wrap That Looks Boutique—Using Simple Supplies

Brown paper. One beautiful ribbon. A little greenery. A tiny bell. That’s the whole secret to a gift that feels thoughtfully styled, without buying expensive specialty wrapping paper.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Wrap it simply. Cover a gift box in plain kraft paper and fold the edges neatly.
  2. Add the statement ribbon. Cross a length of wide burgundy wired ribbon around the package and tie a generous bow.
  3. Tuck in greenery. Slip a small faux evergreen sprig beneath the ribbon; trim it so it stays inside the gift’s edges.
  4. Finish with a bell and tag. Tie on one lightweight decorative bell and a blank kraft tag. Write the name by hand.

The pictured concept can be recreated with these ordinary materials; exact colors and proportions will depend on the items you select. Reuse ribbon, bells and faux greenery when the gifts are opened.
