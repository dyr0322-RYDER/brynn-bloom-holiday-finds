Christmas Gift Wrap That Looks Boutique—Using Simple Supplies

Brown paper. One beautiful ribbon. A little greenery. A tiny bell. That’s the whole secret to a gift that feels thoughtfully styled, without buying expensive specialty wrapping paper.

Shop the budget-friendly supplies ↓

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Wrap it simply. Cover a gift box in plain kraft paper and fold the edges neatly.
  2. Add the statement ribbon. Cross a length of wide burgundy wired ribbon around the package and tie a generous bow.
  3. Tuck in greenery. Tuck a small faux evergreen sprig and a few artificial red berries beneath the ribbon; trim them to sit neatly on top of the gift.
  4. Finish with a bell and tag. Tie on two or three lightweight miniature gold bells and a blank ivory or kraft tag. Write a short message by hand.

The image is an AI-generated styling concept. To recreate the look, choose ribbon wide enough for generous bows, several miniature bells per gift, faux greenery, red berry accents and gift tags. Exact colors and proportions depend on your supplies. Reuse ribbon, bells and faux greenery when the gifts are opened.

BOUTIQUE LOOK · EVERYDAY BUDGET

Make the pictured look for less.

Match the photo, not an expensive shopping cart. The essentials are plain brown kraft paper, wide burgundy velvet-style ribbon, small gold jingle bells, tiny evergreen and berry sprigs, and simple tags. Buy small packs or use materials you already own.

BUDGET BEAUTIFUL

Start with basic kraft paper and one roll of burgundy ribbon. Cut small pieces from a reusable faux greenery stem, add a few berry tips and bells, and make handwritten tags from scrap cardstock. Skip large multipacks if you are wrapping only a few gifts.

DESIGNER-INSPIRED FINISH

Keep the same inexpensive kraft-paper base. Upgrade only the ribbon texture or bell finish for a richer look. The photo’s burgundy ribbon, gold bells and greenery matter more than buying specialty gift wrap.
