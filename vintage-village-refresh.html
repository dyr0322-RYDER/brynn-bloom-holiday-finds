The Vintage Christmas Village Refresh

Make mismatched decorative houses look like a collected winter village.

Original illustrated design concept; not a photograph of a completed project. The final appearance depends on the materials and technique you choose.

The creative shortcut: Start with what you own, change one finish or focal point, and repeat a coordinated detail. No expensive premade decoration required.
THE MAKEOVER

How to create the look

01

Gather the mismatched pieces

Use decorative houses you own or find secondhand. Set aside anything valuable or collectible that you would not want to alter.

02

Choose one quiet palette

Pick two or three tones such as warm ivory, stone and muted burgundy. Keep roof colors consistent.

03

Paint in thin layers

Clean and dry the houses, test adhesion, then paint the exterior with a compatible product. Keep openings clear.

04

Arrange your village

Vary the house heights, add a light layer of faux snow and place suitable battery-operated lights only where safe.
