SEASONAL MAGIC / THE EASY DIY EDIT

Give Mismatched Christmas Village Houses a Beautiful New Look

A little paint and a shared palette can make secondhand holiday houses feel like a collected village.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Gather the houses. Choose inexpensive decorative houses; avoid altering valuable collectibles.
  2. Pick the palette. Use warm ivory, stone and one muted accent color for a coordinated set.
  3. Paint in thin layers. Test adhesion and apply suitable craft paint to clean, dry exteriors.
  4. Arrange and light. Group houses at varied heights and use battery-operated LED tea lights only where safe.

Photograph is styling inspiration, not an exact depiction of the finished DIY. The final look depends on your materials. Keep décor away from flames and heat; use flameless candles where applicable.
