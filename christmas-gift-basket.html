DIY Christmas Gift Basket That Looks Boutique-Made

Create a cozy, personal holiday gift using a small woven basket, a soft throw or hand towel, a mug, hot cocoa and a favorite chocolate treat. Finish with burgundy velvet ribbon, a little greenery and a handwritten gift tag. Perfect for teachers, neighbors, coworkers and friends.

THE LITTLE GIFT

Make the basket in five steps

  1. Choose the basket and soft base. Start with a small, sturdy woven basket. Fold a cream-colored throw, hand towel or soft scarf and tuck it into the back to add height. For a smaller budget, use a pretty tea towel.
  2. Add the main gifts. Place a mug near the front and a sealed hot cocoa mix packet behind it. Add a wrapped chocolate bar or individually wrapped treats. Position taller items toward the back so everything can be seen.
  3. Fill and arrange. Add one or two optional extras, such as a small candle, cookies or a little bag of chocolate. Tuck a faux evergreen-and-berry pick into the back for color; keep decorative greenery away from food and open flames.
  4. Tie a velvet bow. Wrap wide burgundy velvet wired ribbon around the basket or attach a generous bow to its front. Use floral wire or twine to secure the bow if needed.
  5. Add a personal tag. Write “Warm Wishes” or a note for your recipient on a kraft gift tag and tie it beneath the bow. Check that all items are stable before carrying or gifting the basket.

Make it your own

For teachers: a mug, cocoa, a packaged treat and a handwritten thank-you. For neighbors: cocoa, cookies and a tea towel. For friends: a cozy throw, mug and a favorite snack. Skip the candle if you do not know the recipient’s fragrance preferences. Choose packaged treats with visible ingredient and allergen labels.

You do not need to buy every item in the concept image. Choose a basket size that fits your budget and use items you already own when possible. The finished basket will vary with your selections.
