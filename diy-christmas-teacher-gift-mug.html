DIY Christmas Teacher Gift Mug: A Cozy Cocoa Surprise

A festive mug, a few individually wrapped treats, and a pretty ribbon become a thoughtful Christmas gift for a teacher. This is an easy gift to make in multiples, and you can adapt it to what you already have at home.

MAKE THE GIFT

Six simple steps

  1. Choose a mug. Start with a clean, food-safe Christmas mug or a simple solid-color mug. A teacher-themed design is optional. Check for chips or cracks and wash and dry it before assembling.
  2. Add a little filler. Tuck clean crinkle paper or tissue into the bottom to lift the treats above the rim. Keep paper away from any unwrapped food.
  3. Build the cocoa treat bundle. Add one or two sealed hot-cocoa packets, a small sealed bag of marshmallows, and a couple of individually wrapped chocolates. Use a wrapped candy cane if it fits. Check the mug's capacity before shopping so everything can fit comfortably.
  4. Make it festive. Arrange the packets toward the back, with chocolates in front. A tiny faux evergreen sprig can sit outside the food packaging. Avoid glitter or loose craft pieces near food.
  5. Tie a bow. Use red plaid ribbon, velvet ribbon or twine around the mug handle or a clear gift bag. If using a gift bag, choose one large enough for the mug and tie it above the contents.
  6. Add a personal thank-you. Write “Thank you for making a difference every day!” on a gift tag. Have your child sign the back for a more meaningful finish.

Can you make it for under $15?

That is a target budget, not a verified current price. An example spending plan is $4 for a mug, $2 for cocoa, $2 for marshmallows, $3 for wrapped chocolates and a candy cane, and $2 for shared ribbon, filler and a tag: about $13 per gift before tax or shipping. Actual costs vary by store, brand, pack size and how many gifts you make. Reusing ribbon or buying multipacks for several teachers may help keep the cost down.

Keep it food-safe: Leave all edible treats in their original sealed packaging, including ingredient and allergen information. Do not add loose marshmallows or unwrapped chocolate directly to craft filler. Ask about school gift policies and dietary restrictions when appropriate.
