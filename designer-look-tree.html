The Designer-Look Christmas Tree

A full coordinated tree without replacing every ornament.

Inspiration photo: Aymerik Grenier / Pexels. Not a photograph of a product being sold.

Before buying: Compare the listing’s dimensions, materials, quantity, weather suitability and delivery terms. A styled photograph may feature more than one package of greenery, ribbon or ornaments.
STEP 01

Shop your ornament box first

Separate what you own by color and finish. Choose two colors you already have and add one metallic accent. This prevents the common trap of buying a completely new theme every year.

STEP 02

Fluff before you fill

Start near the trunk and bend each branch outward. A thoroughly fluffed tree often looks fuller without buying extra ornament sets or large picks.

STEP 03

Use ribbon in short sections

Tuck small sections into the branches rather than wrapping one continuous spiral. It is easier to adjust, and you can use ribbon left over from gifts.

STEP 04

Work in layers

Put large ornaments farther inside the tree and hang smaller pieces at branch tips. Repeat a shape in several areas so the tree looks intentional.

STEP 05

Measure tree accessories

Check the stand diameter and the widest part of the base before buying a collar or skirt. An attractive but undersized collar is wasted money.
