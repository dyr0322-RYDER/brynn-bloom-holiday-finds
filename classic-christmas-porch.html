The Classic Christmas Porch

An inviting entry layered in evergreen, ribbon, and warm light.

Before buying: Compare the listing’s dimensions, materials, quantity, weather suitability and delivery terms. A styled photograph may feature more than one package of greenery, ribbon or ornaments.
STEP 01

Start with one color story

Evergreen, red and warm white is enough. Repeating these three colors makes separate pieces look like a collection, even when you bought them from different places.

STEP 02

Put the money where people look

A generous wreath is the focal point. Keep its bow to one side if the door is narrow. Use the wreath you already own and replace only its ribbon or add berry picks before shopping for a whole new one.

STEP 03

Measure before buying garland

Measure both sides and the top of your door frame, then compare that total to the length in the listing. Check if garland is outdoor-rated and whether lights are included. Don't assume a photograph shows the quantity you will receive.

STEP 04

Style the lower half

Two lanterns or planters of different heights make an entrance feel layered. One thrifted lantern plus an existing potted evergreen can work just as well as a matching set. Keep the walkway and door swing unobstructed.

STEP 05

Repeat the finishing detail

Use the same ribbon on the wreath, lantern or porch greenery. If the look seems unfinished, try repeating a color before buying another item.
