THE EASY VERSION

Build one jar

  • ½ cup hot cocoa mix
  • ¼ cup mini chocolate chips
  • ½ cup mini marshmallows

Important: Jar sizes vary, so treat these amounts as a starting point and adjust the layers to fit your container. Use commercially packaged shelf-stable ingredients and check ingredient labels for allergens before gifting.

Put it together in four steps

  1. Start clean and dry. Wash the jar and lid and let them dry completely before adding food.
  2. Layer the ingredients. Add cocoa mix first, chocolate chips second and mini marshmallows on top.
  3. Seal and decorate. Close tightly, then add ribbon, a small faux greenery sprig or bell to the outside of the jar.
  4. Add the tag. Print and cut the free tags above. Add a separate preparation note using the directions from your cocoa mix package so the recipient knows how much mix and liquid to use.

Make several at once

Set up an assembly line with jars, cocoa, chips and marshmallows. Measure the same amount into each jar, then decorate them together. This makes the idea especially practical when you need multiple small gifts.

Gift safely: Include an ingredient/allergen note when appropriate. Keep the jars sealed, dry and stored according to the ingredient manufacturers' directions. Decorative bells and small embellishments are not toys and should be kept away from young children.
