I Turned a Thrifted Mirror into a Gorgeous Christmas Vignette!

An arched antique-gold mirror becomes the backdrop for a cozy holiday scene with a full evergreen garland, burgundy velvet and burlap bow, warm fairy lights, flameless candles and a tiny glowing church. Reuse a mirror and decorations you own to keep this makeover affordable.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Style your Christmas mirror vignette

  1. Find your mirror. The image uses a tall arched mirror with a warm antique-gold or bronze wood-look frame. Shop your home or secondhand first. Check the hanging hardware and securely anchor the mirror before adding any décor.
  2. Arrange the greenery. Drape realistic faux pine or cedar garland over the top of the frame and down both sides. Use removable garland ties or suitable hooks that do not scratch the mirror or strain its hanging hardware.
  3. Make the statement bow. Layer a full bow from deep burgundy velvet wired ribbon and natural burlap. Attach it at the top center of the arch. Add short evergreen sprigs behind it to make the topper look lush.
  4. Bring in berries and pinecones. Tuck small glossy red berry picks and mini frosted pinecones into the garland, with a little extra fullness at the upper corners and near the base.
  5. Weave in a warm glow. Wind battery-operated warm-white fairy lights through the garland, securing the wire gently and keeping the battery pack accessible. Avoid dangling cords across the tabletop.
  6. Build the tabletop scene. Place distressed creamwashed wood candle holders in varied heights in front of the mirror and top them with ivory battery-operated LED pillar candles. Set a miniature ivory light-up church and one or two tiny bottle-brush trees to the side, with leftover greenery and berries along the tabletop.
  7. Check the final balance. Leave some mirror visible so it reflects the warm lights. Keep all decorations stable and away from the edge of the table. Enjoy the glow on an entryway console or mantel.

Make the most of your holiday bins

Budget tip: A mirror you already own, a garland from last year, ribbon offcuts and flameless candles can recreate much of the image without buying a new base. The mirror is a backdrop; you can remove the decorations after Christmas.

Safety: Use battery LED candles and lights only, never an open flame near greenery or ribbon. Ensure the mirror is secured and the garland is not pulling it forward. Keep batteries and small decorations away from children and pets.
