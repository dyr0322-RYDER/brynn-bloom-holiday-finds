I Turned Inexpensive Wine Glasses into a Magical Christmas Display!

Turn three ordinary wine glasses upside down and transform the bowls into miniature snowy Christmas scenes. Add lightweight flameless candles to the glass feet, a few twine bows and tiny greenery accents for a showstopping holiday centerpiece.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the wine-glass Christmas display

  1. Choose the right glasses. Use three clear stemmed wine glasses with wide bowls and flat, level feet. Graduated heights create the pictured silhouette. Measure the inside opening and height of each bowl before buying the miniatures.
  2. Create removable snowy scenes. Cut three thin round cardstock or foam-board disks slightly larger than the rims. Add a light layer of faux snow, then arrange a tiny ivory church in the tallest glass scene, a small deer in another and a miniature cottage in the third. Add snowy bottle-brush trees. Keep all decorations inside the outline of each bowl.
  3. Secure the miniatures. Attach trees and houses to the disks with appropriate craft adhesive, following drying instructions. Do not rely on loose artificial snow to hold anything upright. Optional tiny warm-white LEDs can sit in the scene only if their battery packs remain accessible and their wires are not pinched by glass.
  4. Turn each glass into a globe. Place an upside-down glass carefully over its miniature scene and center the rim on the base disk. Secure the disk to the rim with removable clear craft tape or another reversible method, then lift and move the entire piece carefully. The glass bowl is the dome; its flat foot is the candle platform.
  5. Dress the stems and tops. Tie simple jute bows around the stems. Add small faux pine sprigs, red berries and mini frosted pinecones near the feet, keeping the candle platform flat and unobstructed.
  6. Add flameless candlelight. Set one lightweight, appropriately sized ivory battery-operated LED candle on each flat foot. Check that the candle stands securely and does not overhang. Never use real flames on an inverted glass.
  7. Display as a trio. Arrange all three on a stable rustic tray, tallest in the middle, and add a little loose greenery around the bases. Keep them away from table edges, children and pets.

Make it for less

Budget tip: Reuse three glasses from your cabinet and miniature ornaments from last year's decorations. One pack of bottle-brush trees and one bag of artificial snow can make all three scenes. Skip the optional tray if you already own one.

Safety: Battery-operated LED candles only—never real candles or flames on glass feet. Do not move the glasses by their stems while decorated. Keep the display stable and keep button batteries, loose snow and miniatures away from children and pets.
