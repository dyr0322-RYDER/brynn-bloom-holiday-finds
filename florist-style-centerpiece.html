SEASONAL MAGIC / THE EASY DIY EDIT

A Florist-Style Christmas Centerpiece from an Everyday Bowl

Create a low, lush holiday arrangement with faux greenery, a few berry picks and flameless candles.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Choose the vessel. Start with a shallow bowl or tray you already own.
  2. Set the candles. Arrange flameless pillars of varied heights in the center.
  3. Layer greenery. Build outward with artificial pine stems and floral wire where needed.
  4. Finish sparingly. Cluster berry picks and pinecones, leaving room on the table for guests.

Photograph is styling inspiration, not an exact depiction of the finished DIY. The final look depends on your materials. Keep décor away from flames and heat; use flameless candles where applicable.
