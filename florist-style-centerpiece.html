The Florist-Style Christmas Centerpiece

A lush-looking low centerpiece built around a dish you already own.

Original illustrated design concept; not a photograph of a completed project. The final appearance depends on the materials and technique you choose.

The creative shortcut: Start with what you own, change one finish or focal point, and repeat a coordinated detail. No expensive premade decoration required.
THE MAKEOVER

How to create the look

01

Choose a low vessel

Start with a shallow bowl or tray that leaves room for place settings and conversation.

02

Set the focal point

Place flameless candles of varied heights, leaving access to switches and batteries.

03

Build the greenery outward

Layer artificial pine stems from the center toward the edges; use floral wire where needed.

04

Finish with a few accents

Add berries and pinecones in small groups. Stop when the arrangement looks full from seated eye level.
