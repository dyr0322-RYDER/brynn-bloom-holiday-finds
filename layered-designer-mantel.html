SEASONAL MAGIC / THE EASY DIY EDIT

A Full-Looking Christmas Mantel with Simple Garland

The secret is not expensive premade garland. It is layering basic greenery, a few stems and repeating one ribbon color.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Measure and plan. Choose a safe location away from flames, hot surfaces and vents; measure the width.
  2. Build a base. Secure one strand of simple artificial garland using suitable fasteners.
  3. Add volume. Tuck filler stems into sparse areas, especially around the focal point.
  4. Finish thoughtfully. Add warm battery-operated lights and a few coordinated ribbon accents.

Photograph is styling inspiration, not an exact depiction of the finished DIY. The final look depends on your materials. Keep décor away from flames and heat; use flameless candles where applicable.
