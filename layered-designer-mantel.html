CHRISTMAS STYLING · SMART LAYERING

The Layered Designer Mantel

Build a full-looking Christmas mantel from simple greenery and repeated accents.

Original illustrated design concept; not a photograph of a completed project. The final appearance depends on the materials and technique you choose.

The creative shortcut: Start with what you own, change one finish or focal point, and repeat a coordinated detail. No expensive premade decoration required.
THE MAKEOVER

How to create the look

01

Measure the mantel

Measure its width and decide where greenery can drape without blocking heat vents, flames or access.

02

Build a simple base

Position one strand of plain garland and secure it with appropriate hooks or ties.

03

Add depth where it counts

Tuck individual evergreen stems toward the front and ends. Concentrate extra fullness near the focal point.

04

Repeat the finishing details

Weave in warm lights and add two or three coordinated ribbon accents instead of buying a fully decorated garland.
