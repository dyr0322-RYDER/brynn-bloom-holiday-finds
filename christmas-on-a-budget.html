BRYNN & BLOOM · CHRISTMAS AT HOME

Christmas Décor on a Budget

Recreate an expensive-looking holiday home with thoughtful low-cost swaps.

Before buying: Compare the listing’s dimensions, materials, quantity, weather suitability and delivery terms. A styled photograph may feature more than one package of greenery, ribbon or ornaments.
STEP 01

Treat the picture as a recipe, not a shopping list

When an inspiration image catches your eye, identify its three visual ingredients: palette, focal point and texture. You don't need to buy every object pictured to create a similar feeling.

STEP 02

Spend on one visible focal point

For a porch, that could be a wreath. For a living room, a tree or mantel. Use plain inexpensive garland and improve it with ribbon, picks or lights you already own.

STEP 03

Use the ribbon trick

Matching bows make mixed greenery look coordinated. Buy one spool only after measuring how many bows you need; wired ribbon can be reshaped and reused.

STEP 04

Combine inexpensive and existing pieces

Fill out a sparse wreath with a few berry stems. Place an existing throw beneath two seasonal pillow covers. Use ordinary glass jars as a small centerpiece with flameless candles.

STEP 05

Set a cap before browsing

Decide your maximum for the entire look, then make a short list of missing items. Compare pack quantities, measurements and delivery costs—not just the price shown at the top of the listing.
