Paper Towel Holder → Christmas Candle

Start with an ordinary wooden paper towel holder and transform it into dramatic Christmas decor with paint, a flameless candle, greenery, ribbon and bells.

THE TRANSFORMATION

How to create the look

  1. Start with the holder. Choose a sturdy freestanding wooden paper towel holder.
  2. Paint it. Apply black or oil-rubbed bronze paint and let it dry fully.
  3. Add the candle. Secure a lightweight battery-operated flameless pillar candle at the top.
  4. Add greenery. Arrange faux evergreen and berry picks around the upper post.
  5. Add the bow. Attach a full wired plaid bow beneath the greenery.
  6. Finish it. Add lightweight gold bells, small pinecones and optional faux snow.
  7. Style it. Display indoors or in a dry, covered decorative area.

Safety: Use only a battery-operated flameless candle—never a real flame. Follow paint and adhesive directions, and keep small decorative pieces away from children and pets.

Why it works

The tall center post already gives you the silhouette of a candle stand. A dark finish disguises the ordinary starting object while the candle and oversized Christmas embellishments create the wow.
