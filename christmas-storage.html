Christmas Storage That Makes January Easier

Spend less time untangling Christmas next year.

Before buying: Compare the listing’s dimensions, materials, quantity, weather suitability and delivery terms. A styled photograph may feature more than one package of greenery, ribbon or ornaments.
STEP 01

Store by decorating zone

Use clear labels such as tree, porch, mantel and table. When you reopen the bins, the contents match how you actually decorate.

STEP 02

Measure the largest pieces

Large wreaths, tree toppers and oversized ornaments may not fit standard organizers. Measure first rather than ordering storage from a styled photograph.

STEP 03

Protect the fragile things

Use dividers or reused packaging to separate breakable ornaments. Keep delicate ribbons from being crushed beneath heavy decorations.

STEP 04

Keep lights organized

Wind light strands neatly and label the location each set belongs to. Test strands before putting them away.

STEP 05

Make an inventory note

A photo of each open bin helps you see what you own before next year's holiday shopping begins.
