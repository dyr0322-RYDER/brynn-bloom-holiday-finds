SEASONAL MAGIC / THE EASY DIY EDIT

A Vintage-Look Christmas Bell from a Simple Decoration

Turn a plain decorative bell into an heirloom-inspired accent with a soft metallic finish and a generous ribbon.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in four steps

  1. Clean and prep. Choose a lightweight decorative bell you already own; clean and dry it and check that the finish is suitable for craft paint.
  2. Paint gently. Apply thin layers of compatible metallic craft paint according to its label, allowing each coat to dry.
  3. Age the finish. Test a tiny amount of antiquing wax on a hidden spot, then wipe the raised areas to reveal the metallic highlights.
  4. Add the finishing touch. Tie on burgundy ribbon and a small faux greenery sprig; use secure hanging hardware.

Photograph is styling inspiration, not an exact depiction of the finished DIY. The final look depends on your materials. Keep décor away from flames and heat; use flameless candles where applicable.
