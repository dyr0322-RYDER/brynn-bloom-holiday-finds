SEASONAL MAGIC / THE EASY DIY EDIT

A Vintage-Look Christmas Bell from a Simple Decoration

Make a statement with one large aged-brass-look bell, an oversized burgundy velvet bow, cascading evergreen greenery, red berries and pinecones. Hang it on a sturdy front-door hook or use it as a mantel accent.

THE LITTLE TRANSFORMATION

Make the look in five steps

  1. Choose a statement bell. Start with one large lightweight decorative metal bell (not a tiny jingle bell). Choose an aged-brass finish, or use a paintable plain bell. Check its weight and hanging loop before decorating.
  2. Create the aged finish, if needed. On a paint-compatible bell, apply thin coats of antique-gold craft paint according to the label. Once dry, test a small amount of dark antiquing wax in a hidden spot and buff gently for a worn-brass effect. Skip painting if your bell already has the right finish.
  3. Make an oversized bow. Form generous loops with wide burgundy velvet or velvet-look wired ribbon, secure the center with floral wire, and leave two long tails that hang past the bell.
  4. Build the greenery cluster. Layer several faux cedar, fir or pine stems around the top of the bell, letting some cascade down the sides. Tuck in faux red berry picks and a few small pinecones; attach securely with floral wire.
  5. Assemble and hang. Secure the bow above the bell, add two small gold accent bells if desired, and hang the finished piece from hardware rated for its weight. Keep it clear of heat sources and door swing.
