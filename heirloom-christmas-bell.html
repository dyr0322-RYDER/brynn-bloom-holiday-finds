The Heirloom Christmas Bell

Turn a plain decorative bell into a warm, collected-looking holiday accent.

Original illustrated design concept; not a photograph of a completed project. The final appearance depends on the materials and technique you choose.

The creative shortcut: Start with what you own, change one finish or focal point, and repeat a coordinated detail. No expensive premade decoration required.
THE MAKEOVER

How to create the look

01

Start with a bell you already have

Look for a lightweight decorative bell at home or secondhand. Wipe it clean and check the finish before painting.

02

Create a mellow metallic finish

On a suitable, well-ventilated work surface, apply a thin layer of metallic craft paint according to its label. Let it dry completely.

03

Add the antique character

Lightly work a small amount of antiquing wax into creases and wipe the high points. Test the finish in a hidden spot first.

04

Make one dramatic ribbon moment

Tie a generous bow at the top, add a small evergreen sprig, and hang where it will not obstruct the door or walkway.
